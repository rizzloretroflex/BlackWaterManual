BLACKWATER: The Manual





THE BRIDGE
The Bridge is the Central Command Post by which you will issue most of your commands.  Set Carrier direction, launch skiffs and drones, configure your economy, research and mining priorities and monitor ship status.

THE BRIDGE

 DRONE CONTROL PANEL
 SKIFF CONTROL PANEL



The Bridge is where you can monitor several systems at once.  At a glance you can see which drones are airborne, which skiffs are launched and the return statuses of each.  Mounted monitors show both current inventory levels as well as digital notifications.



 Drones 1 through 4 are represented on the Drone Control Panel as well as their current state indicated by the individual indicator lights.  The green In the Carrier Bay [BAY], the red Airborne [ABRN] indicator and the blue blinking Returning to the Carrier [RTN].
 Skiffs 1 through 4 are represented on the Skiff Control Panel and their current state represented by their individual indicator lights.  The green In Dock [DOCK] indicator, the red Launched indicator [OUT] and blinking blue Returning to Carrier [RTN] indicator lights.
CARRIER CONTROL PANEL
 INVENTORY AND STATUS SCREEN
 NOTIFICATION SCREEN



The Carrier Control Panel allows you to set the direction and speed of the entire Carrier as well set the Carrier into Automatic or Manual Control.  You can also configure the Resupply Drone to outfit your carrier with whatever items you have stockpiled on the Home Island that need to be brought onto the Carrier.
 The Inventory and Status Screen shows Carrier Health, fuel and the quantities of other items stored on the ship.  Missiles, UAVs, Flares, Long-Range Transmitters and more are represented.  At-a-Glance you can determine how many skiffs and drones are deployed and the number of islands you currently occupy.
The Digital Notification Screen will keep you up-to-date on the current statuses of the carrier, drones, skiffs and near-by threats as they emerge.

There are currently 17 items that can be manufactured at your various Factory Islands and can be given various priorities depending on your strategy and number of Factory islands you currently have.
Drones
 Manufacture Replacement Drones
Skiffs
 Manufacture Replacement Skiffs
AtA/AtG Missiles
 Drone-launched Air-to-Air Missiles/Air-to-Ground missiles
UAVs
 Unmanned Ariel Vehicles transmit images back to the carrier and allows the targeting of Cruise Missiles
Cruise Missiles
 Fired from your Home Base, the Cruise Missile is targeted by the UAV targeting system dealing a great deal of damage
Ball Turret*
 An amphibious offensive/defensive weapon primarly used to patrol your islands and engage threats
Mark V Ball Turret*
 Based on the same model is the original Ball Turret, however the Mark V version is a more robust turret with stronger offensive capabilities and an electronic shield offering greater protection from incoming fire
Flares*
 Acting as a counter-measure against incoming missiles, the Flare distracts and redirects incoming missiles
Anti-Aircraft Gun*
 The Anti-Aircraft Gun fires a series of electronic bolts designed as a deterrant for Drones but can also be effective against Skiffs
Surface-to-Air Missile Site [SAM]*
 A ground-based airborne threat mitigation system only useful against Drones
Flame Thrower*
 A secondary, Skiff-mounted weapon for use against Tactical Droids primarily
Building Pod*
 A Building Pod is a Drone-Carried, parachuting Pod which will destroy any existing infrastructure around its drop location and automatically build whatever preconfigured structure it was programmed to:  A University, Mine or Factory.
Long-Range Transmitter*
 Long-Range Transmitters extend the range of your Drones and Skiffs from the Carrier.  Normal range is 2500 from the Carrier
Plasma Engine Upgrade*
 Doubles the speed of your Carrier from 16kts to 32kts
Transport Portal*
 Allows you to instantly teleport to other regions of the map
Carrier Rail Gun*
 A powerful Deck-mounted gun capable of striking targets at far distance



* - Denotes a Research Requirement


Your Mining Islands produce 3 types of fuel for the Carrier, the Drones and Skiffs.  These items are also prioritized given your configuration and the number of Mining islands you currently have.
Universities provide the knowledge production to research items that will become available to manufacture.  The speed at which these discoveries are made depends on the number of University Islands you have and each individual items research priority.
