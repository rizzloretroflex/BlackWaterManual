Tactical Systems & Operations Manual

The Bridge

The Bridge is the Central Command Post by which you will issue most of your commands. Set Carrier direction, launch skiffs and drones, configure your economy, research and mining priorities, and monitor ship status.

Bridge Subsystems

Key monitoring, control, and response panels available on the main bridge command desk.

The Bridge

Monitor several systems at once. At a glance, view active airborne drones, launched skiffs, and return statuses. Mounted monitors show live inventory levels and real-time digital notifications.

Drone Control Panel

Drones 1 through 4 are represented with individual indicator lights: Green for In Carrier Bay [BAY], Red for Airborne [ABRN], and blinking Blue for Returning [RTN].

Skiff Control Panel

Skiffs 1 through 4 status tracking via dedicated indicator lights: Green for In Dock [DOCK], Red for Launched [OUT], and blinking Blue for Returning [RTN].

Carrier Control Panel

Set carrier speed, direction, and toggle between Automatic or Manual Control. Configure the Resupply Drone to bring stockpiled items from Home Island directly onto the ship.

Inventory & Status Screen

Displays Carrier health, fuel, and storage quantities (Missiles, UAVs, Flares, Transmitters). Quickly check deployed units and the total number of currently occupied islands.

Notification Screen

Digital feed providing real-time operational status updates across the carrier, drones, skiffs, and emerging nearby threats.

Mining Islands extract 3 essential fuel types for the Carrier, Drones, and Skiffs. Fuel allocation and extraction rates are prioritized based on active Mining Island count and user settings.

Tactical Operations

The Tac View

A consolidated tactical command display merging Bridge sub-systems with an interactive map. Used for plotting waypoints, launching UAVs, configuring economy priorities, and accessing Island Views.

The Island Panel & Targeting

Allows drag-and-drop tactical positioning for defensive items across controlled islands. Position assets behind natural terrain features to shield them from incoming enemy carrier deck artillery.

Cruise Missiles

Launched directly from your Home Island stockpile, Cruise Missiles lock onto and strike targets designated by active UAV targeting reticles.

Environmental Threats

Cyclones

Wandering sea-based storms that move unpredictably. Cyclones damage friendly and enemy assets alike, destroying defensive weapons, factories, mines, or universities in their path.

Ion Plasma Sentries

Autonomous sentries deployed from the Enemy Fortress. They patrol combat zones and instantly obliterate any unit entering their forward detection cone.
